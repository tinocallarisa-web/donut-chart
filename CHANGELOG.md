# Changelog — Donut & Pie Chart by TCViz

## 1.0.0.0 — 25 November 2026
- First release: donut or pie with inner radius, start angle, Space between slices, Border and order by value or by the data.
- Data labels inside the slices, all with the same content and the same text size; a label that does not fit is left out, never cut; outside with leader lines when there is no legend or table.
- Percentages rounded by largest remainder, so labels, table and total row add up to 100.
- An honest Other (Show at most, Group below %) that opens in the data table; selecting it filters by every item inside it, up to 1,000 items.
- Total in the center; the name, value and percent of the slice you point at, select or focus; the sum of a multiple selection.
- Legend and Data table, independent, each with its own position; table columns, header, total row, gridlines, banding and sorting by header.
- Summary sentence above the chart and Footnotes for excluded values and incomplete rows.
- Scale ring at 25/50/75% or every 10%; a selectable font in every text of the visual.
- Pro: Compare with — Δ pp with a mini bar, Δ %, new and gone, a ⚠ where share and value move in opposite directions, the change in the labels, Sort by change.
- Pro: Target — % of target with ✓ ≈ ✕, Met at and Close at, Status on slices.
- Pro: Segment — an outer ring with its own labels and segment rows in the table; the six largest segments of each category and its own Other (n); a note when the two fields are nested the wrong way round.
- Pro: Annotations in the table and the tooltip; Small multiples with a shared table of the total and Size by total; six collection style templates.
- Pro preview: while editing without a licence, a limited preview under a watermark — Pro data for the three largest categories only, the first 4 small multiples panels, collection styles whole; reading view shows the free chart.
- Lasso selection, Drilldown, cross-filtering, highlight (Other included), bookmarks, multi-visual selection, Report page tooltips, context menu, keyboard navigation.
- Accessibility card: high contrast, patterns, focus colour and width, minimum text size, reduce motion.
- Number format of the measure, Dynamic format strings included; Conditional formatting (fx) on slice colours, border and table header; On-object formatting; 12 languages.
- Up to 30,000 rows per block, with up to 10 more blocks requested from Power BI; a footnote when rows are missing.
- At least the min(5, Show at most − 1) largest categories always have a name; selections above 1,000 items or rows are shown inside the visual only, with a note; while editing, a suggestion when a donut is not the form for the data.
- Report theme and Classic styles, setup guide, start screen in three sizes. No network requests and no privileges.
