WHAT TO CHECK (sample .pbix, table Ventas)
1. Empty visual: start screen in three sizes, no links.
2. Category = Marca, Values = Litros: labels inside, total in the center, Other (5).
Format > Data table > Position = Right: Other (5) opens with a click.
3. Pro: Compare with = Litros AA (Δ pp, Δ %, ⚠, summary), Target = Plan litros (✓ ≈ ✕),
Segment = Canal (outer ring), Small multiples = Zona, Annotation = Nota (First).
4. Click a slice, legend item or table row: other visuals filter (Other passes every item
in it). Ctrl+click adds; drag a lasso on the chart. Bookmarks restore the selection.
Highlight from another visual. Hierarchy in Category: drilldown.
5. Keyboard: Tab, arrows, Enter, Ctrl+Enter, Esc, Shift+F10. High contrast. Format mode:
click slices, labels, center, legend, table or ring (on-object).

PERSISTPROPERTIES
Only on a user click in the setup guide (style.guide, style.template) and once when the
author changes Style template in the Format pane. Never on selection or in the select callback.

ROWS
Categorical mapping, window of 30,000; fetchMoreData, up to 10 more segments (tested in Desktop
with 54,662 rows). A footnote says when rows are missing. Selections above 1,000 identities
(items or rows) are drawn locally only, with a note; select() is not called, no filter. Segment: 6 largest segments per category + Other (n).
